About Me

My journey as a software developer began when I decided to overcome my fears and pursue my dream of entering the IT world.
Before starting my apprenticeship as an electrician, I always wanted to be an IT professional, imagining myself hacking or modding games.
However, at 14 finding an apprenticeship was difficult, so I chose a path close to technology, becoming an electrician.

After completing my 4-year apprenticeship, I worked for three more years, taking on roles like Construction Manager, handling projects, and supporting larger ones.
Despite my achievements, I felt unfulfilled. I eventually realized I had buried my childhood dream of becoming an IT professional deep within me.

On August 24, 2023, I applied and began studying at WISS Schule für Wirtschaft Informatik Immobilien while starting an internship at SoftART.
This was a turning point in my journey as a developer.

At SoftART, I learned to prioritize software design before coding and gained valuable insights into scalable, structured development.
My experience with Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) and the teachings of Juval Löwy significantly shaped my mindset and skills, elevating me to a professional level.

I’m grateful for the mentorship and moments that have defined my path in software development.bly grateful for the mentorship and the countless moments that shaped me into the developer I am today.

Internship Project
Multi Calculator

During my internship at SoftART,
I developed a calculator project in C# using WPF (Windows Presentation Foundation).
The project followed a Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) and utilized the MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) pattern.
It also supports computing and displaying various functions with the OxyPlot framework.
